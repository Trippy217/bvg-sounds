About Us

BGV Sounds is a full-service audio house for artists, brands, and creators who need sound that holds up outside the studio — sound that still performs in a mix, behind a livestream, or across a campaign. Our work spans four connected areas: music production, sound branding, studio recording, and background vocals. We treat arrangement, vocal texture, mix, and identity as one decision made early, not four patched together later — a standard that holds whether we're building a full track from scratch or adding a single vocal layer to an existing mix. Our clients range from independent artists shaping a debut to established acts, brands whose audio needs to match their visual identity, and other studios bringing us in for one specific piece. What ties it together is attention to detail a listener won't consciously notice, but will feel. On production, we handle a track's full lifecycle — concept through mastering — shaped by the project, not a house sound. Sound branding builds recognizable audio identities: sonic logos, jingles, consistent sound across every touchpoint. Our studio handles everything from solo vocal takes to full-band sessions. And our BGV team adds the harmony and texture that make a track feel finished.

What we do

Recording & Production – tracking vocals/instruments, mixing, and mastering for artists and labels.

Sound Branding / Audio Identity – custom sonic logos, jingles, and brand soundscapes for companies and advertising.

Voiceover & Session Vocals – background vocals, ADR, podcast narration, and commercial voiceover work.

Background Vocals – trained voices adding harmony, depth, and texture to any mix.

Sound Branding – sonic logos, jingles, and audio identity for brands and businesses.

Live Sound & Recording – capturing live performances, concerts, or events, plus live mixing/engineering.
