FAQ

What's included in an hourly rate?

Your hourly rate covers studio time, a studio engineer, and access to the backlines listed for that session type. Additional gear beyond the standard backline attracts extra charges.

Do you offer overnight sessions?

Yes, overnight sessions can be arranged. Reach out to discuss availability and rates for extended or late-night bookings.
